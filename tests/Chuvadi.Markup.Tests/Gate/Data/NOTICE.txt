Frozen test data for the Chuvadi.Markup clean-room gate.

- Expected outputs were produced by running Chuvadi.Markup 1.1.0 (unreleased; the library before this rewrite,
  commit 349ee73, with the approved MaxNodes rule R5-01 applied to its results, and with the approved D-141 rule
  applied: U+000B, U+000C, U+00A0 and U+2028 are not blank, so a paragraph made only of them is kept and an ATX
  heading keeps a leading or trailing U+000B or U+000C, 15 fuzz cases; and D-143: a paragraph or setext heading
  keeps a leading or trailing U+000C, 46 more fuzz cases; and D-157: an extended www. autolink also starts after
  U+000B, 10 more fuzz cases; and D-174: link labels keep U+000B, U+000C and U+00A0 when normalized, with the
  given library's Markdown/Blocks.cs changed to match and 41 label cases added; and D-199: 3 cases for the
  unsafe-URL rule's data:image exception added; and D-232/D-237: the rulings of patch 7 applied and 3,491 cases
  added: 2,086 minimal and 1,379 longer inputs from my comparison generators (seeds 20261106001 to 20261106005,
  shrunk automatically) and 26 heading-id inputs I wrote; and D-242: 1,336 more cases on the domain start, 1,330
  from the same comparison and 6 I wrote) as a
  program over the inputs. They are data, not code.
- Spec inputs come from the bundled specification files (see specs/LICENSES.md).
